WHO WE ARE

Solving Everyday Parking Problems

ParkShare is a smart parking-sharing platform designed to connect drivers looking for parking with people who have unused parking spaces.

Our aim is to make finding a parking space simple while creating an opportunity for parking space owners to earn from their available spaces.
